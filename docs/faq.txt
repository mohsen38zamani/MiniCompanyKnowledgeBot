FAQ - ParsCRM

Q: What is ParsCRM?
A: ParsCRM is a lightweight customer relationship management platform built for small and medium businesses in Iran.

Q: Which teams can use ParsCRM?
A: Sales, support, operations, and customer success teams can use it.

Q: Does ParsCRM support Persian language?
A: Product UI supports Persian and English. For this technical test, only English content is used.

Q: How is customer data organized?
A: Data is organized by accounts, contacts, deals, and support tickets.

Q: Is there a free trial?
A: Yes, there is a 14-day trial with all core features enabled.
