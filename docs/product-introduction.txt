Product Introduction - ParsCRM

ParsCRM helps Iranian distributors and service companies manage customer lifecycle from lead capture to post-sale support.

Core modules:
- Lead and pipeline management
- Contact and account history
- Ticketing and SLA tracking
- Team activity reporting

MVP scope for this project:
- Read company documents from local docs folder
- Let user ask a question in a simple web interface
- Return grounded answer based only on local documents

Non-goals for MVP:
- Multi-tenant auth
- External vector database
- Billing and subscription management
